UNDERSTAND

Understand the operational problem

Observe real frontline workflows. Pinpoint where data gets lost, why spreadsheets break, and where human manual delay creates operational bottlenecks.

02
STRUCTURE

Define the schema, data flow, validation constraints, and user access roles. Establish clean single-source-of-truth architectures before writing code.

03
